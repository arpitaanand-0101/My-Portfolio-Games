Ultimate Crystal Battlegrounds ✧
A premium, retro-minimalist suite of multi-dimensional Tic-Tac-Toe variants. Built entirely using vanilla JavaScript, CSS variables, and fluid vector layouts, this dashboard transforms a classic pastime into an atmospheric grid strategy simulator.

The aesthetic trades harsh blinding neon for a highly polished, moody, dark-obsidian and glassmorphic canvas, optimized for crisp grid scaling and tactical gameplay.

    Game Engines Supported
Select your operational matrix profile via the configuration drop-down menu inside the cabinet viewport:

Classic 3x3: The pure legacy coordinate battleground. Complete with dynamic turn indicators.

Mega 4x4 Matrix: Expanded matrix canvas where players must coordinate 4 sequential vector matches horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to declare victory.

3D Stacking Plane: Deep operational tier routing across three distinct stacking dimensional layers. Win by completing standard lines on an individual plane, or driving cross-cutting vectors vertically through Layer 1, Layer 2, and Layer 3 simultaneously.

Inception Mode (Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe): A master 3x3 board where every single square contains its own macro-integrated 3x3 miniature matrix. Your structural placement maps the precise grid zone where your opponent is forced to play next. Win individual mini-boards to capture the master sectors and conquer the board!

      Visual & Technical Design Architecture
Glassmorphic Plate Grid Systems: Eliminates standard block lines in favor of high-end translucent glass panels (rgba back-plates) backed by structural slate edges.

Intricate Vector Shards: Heavy-weight vector graphics displaying custom mathematical shapes. Features a multi-faceted Ruby Diamond Shard (X) and dual-concentric precision orbit Sapphire Rings (O) with soft element dropshadow glows.

Synth Sound Synthesis Engine: No heavy audio asset baggage. Utilizes clean, lightweight browser native AudioContext and oscillators to generate immediate synthetic audio feedback tones upon click registration.

Integrated AI Automation Engine: Toggle dynamically between interactive Local 2-Player mode and automated artificial opponent testing profiles.
